# FlouSi — Data Deletion Request ## How to delete your data FlouSi is a personal finance tracking app developed by Yogaga. ### 1. Delete data stored on your device To delete your locally stored FlouSi data: 1. Open FlouSi. 2. Go to the app settings. 3. Choose **Delete all data**. 4. Confirm the deletion. This removes the app data stored locally on that device, including expenses, budgets, goals, categories, recurring expenses, and settings. Deletion is permanent. If you use FlouSi on multiple devices, repeat these steps on each device. ### 2. Request help with data deletion If you need help or have a question about data deletion, email: **flousi.support@gmail.com** Use the subject: **FlouSi Data Deletion Request**. Please explain your request without sending passwords, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information. ### 3. Data types and retention - **Local app data:** Your financial records and settings are stored on your device. Use the in-app Delete all data option to remove them from that device. - **Receipt images:** If you saved a receipt image separately in your phone's photos or files, delete that copy separately. - **Third-party advertising data:** When advertising services are enabled, Google Mobile Ads may process certain device, advertising, interaction, or diagnostic data under Google's policies. FlouSi cannot directly delete data retained by Google. Please consult [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information and available controls. FlouSi does not require users to create an account and does not maintain a central database of locally stored expenses. Requests sent by email are handled to respond to the request and may be retained as reasonably necessary for that purpose or legal obligations. ### Contact **App:** FlouSi **Developer:** Yogaga **Email:** flousi.support@gmail.com **Privacy policy:** https://yogaga-dev.github.io/flousi-privacy-policy/ Last updated: October 12, 2026.
